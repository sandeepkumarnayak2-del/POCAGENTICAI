# VPN Troubleshooting

If the corporate VPN stops working after a laptop or operating system update, first verify internet connectivity. Restart the VPN client and the laptop. Check that the VPN client is on the approved company version. If credentials are rejected, do not share passwords with the service desk; use the approved password-reset procedure. If the problem continues after these checks, create a helpdesk ticket with the client version, operating system version, and time of failure.
